## 🚀 Engineering Projects

### ☀️ Portable Solar-Powered Fuel Quality Screening System

- Developed an ESP32-based prototype for screening fuel samples for abnormal properties.
- Integrated sensing components and a portable solar-powered supply concept.
- Designed to support preliminary screening rather than definitive fuel certification.

**Technologies:** ESP32 · Sensors · Embedded Systems · Solar Power

### 🌞 Solar Tracking System

- Designed a single-axis solar tracking system using light sensors.
- Planned DC motor positioning with encoder feedback.
- Applying PID control concepts to regulate motor movement and improve tracking.

**Technologies:** Embedded Systems · DC Motor · Encoder Feedback · PID Control

### 🔢 Digital Pattern Detection System

- Designed a digital circuit to detect a predefined binary pattern.
- Integrated a pseudo-random sequence generator, parallel-in serial-out shift register, and Mealy finite-state machine.
- Simulated and developed the digital logic design.

**Technologies:** Digital Logic · Finite-State Machines · Shift Registers · Proteus · EasyEDA
